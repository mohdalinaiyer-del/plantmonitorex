      Biography
      About the creator

      The owner of Plant Monitor is a 13‑year‑old student named Mohamed Ali Naiyer. He studies at Merryland International School.

      Mohamed began learning Arduino when he built a solar tracker for the SDG Science Exhibition. Since then, he has developed a strong passion for electronics, robotics, and smart systems.

      His hobbies include solving Rubik’s cubes, reading, and working on robotics projects. His favorite subjects are Math, Computer Science, and Physics. Mohamed’s dream is to become a software engineer in the future.

      About This Website
      What Plant Monitor can do

      This website is the official dashboard for the Plant Monitor system.

      It allows users to:

      • View live soil moisture readings
      • View live soil temperature readings
      • Track plant health over time
      • Adjust system settings such as the dry threshold
      • Connect directly to the Arduino using USB
      • View real‑time graphs of sensor data
      • Monitor global climate trends
      • Play a plant‑care mini‑game and compete on a leaderboard
